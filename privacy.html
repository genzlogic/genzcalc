Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

In short: GenzCalc works 100% offline. It does not collect, store on our servers, or share your personal information.

1. Who we are

GenzCalc ("the app", "we", "us") is an all-in-one calculator app for iPhone, developed by YOUR NAME / COMPANY NAME.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, and it does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

3. Data stored on your device

To make the app work, the following is saved locally on your device only:

  • Your calculation history, including the inputs and results.
  • Your favorite calculators.
  • Your settings: theme, default currency, number format and decimal precision.

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete history items or clear all history inside the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of this data.

GenzCalc does not need an internet connection. It does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data, because we do not have it. If you choose to copy or share a result using the app's copy or share buttons, that content goes only where you send it, using your device's share options. Those destinations have their own privacy policies.

6. Children's privacy

GenzCalc does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Security

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security features, such as your passcode and encryption.
